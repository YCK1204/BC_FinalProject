★★★　How to use in RPG Maker MZ　★★★

1. From the MZ Demo folder, copy all the contents from the "effects" folder (including the "Model" and "Texture" folders) into your project's "effects" folder.
2. Open up both the MZ Demo and your project in RPG Maker MZ, within Database > Animations, copy each animation from the Demo into your project.
